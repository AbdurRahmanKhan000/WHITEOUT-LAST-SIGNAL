# Asset License Ledger (SRS NFR-05, section 14.7)

Every bundled model, texture, animation, sound, music and font must be
listed here with creator, source, license, allowed use and local filename
before it ships in a public release. No asset may be added on the basis of
an unrecorded or unverified license.

The concept images supplied with the SRS are AI-generated visual
inspiration only. They are NOT production assets and are NOT shipped
in-game. Production characters must be game-ready, rigged, optimized,
tested, and recorded in this ledger first.
